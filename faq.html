Quick answers

Plumbing questions and answers

Straight answers about how we work, what things cost and what to do before we get there. If your question isn't here, call and ask.

Emergencies and hours

How fast can you get here in an emergency?

Usually within an hour. Across the Austin metro, the average time from your call to our knock on the door is about 45 minutes, day or night. Farther suburbs like Georgetown and Leander can run closer to an hour.

Do you answer the phone 24/7?

Yes. Our own dispatch team in Austin answers every call, any hour, holidays included. You won't get an answering service or a voicemail box.

Do you charge more at night or on weekends?

No. A 2 a.m. call on a holiday costs the same as a weekday afternoon. You get a flat price for the job before any work starts.

What should I do while I wait for the plumber?

Shut off the water. For a burst pipe or big leak, close the main shutoff, which is usually in the meter box by the curb or where the line enters the house. For a leaking water heater, close the cold water valve on top and turn off its power or gas. Keep people and pets away from standing water near outlets. Our emergency guide has step-by-step instructions.

Prices and payment

How much does a plumber cost in Austin?

It depends on the job. Small repairs like a running toilet or a faucet usually cost $125 to $375. Clearing a single drain runs $125 to $250. A new 40 or 50 gallon gas water heater is about $1,600 to $2,600 installed. Each service page lists typical prices, and you always get an exact quote before we start.

Are estimates free?

Yes, for scheduled work. We look at the problem, explain your options and give you a written price. There's no charge if you decide not to go ahead.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, on jobs over $1,000, like water heaters, repipes and sewer work. Financing is through a third-party lender and depends on credit approval. Ask about it when you call.

How can I pay?

Cash, check and all major credit and debit cards. Payment is due when the job is done, unless you're using financing.

Licensing and warranty

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our founder holds a Texas Master Plumber License M-00000 from the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, and every job is run by a licensed plumber. We carry $2 million in general liability insurance and workers' comp on every employee.

What does your warranty cover?

Our labor is guaranteed for one year. If something we repaired or installed fails because of our work, we come back and fix it at no charge. Parts and fixtures carry the manufacturer's warranty, which is often 6 to 10 years on a water heater.

Service area

What areas do you serve?

All of Austin, plus Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Leander, Kyle, Buda, Manor and Lakeway. The service areas page lists ZIP codes and typical response times.

Do you do commercial plumbing?

We work on homes, rental properties and small businesses like offices, shops and restaurants. We don't take on large commercial construction projects.

Repairs and services

Do you work on all water heater brands?

Yes. We repair and install Rheem, A. O. Smith, Bradford White, State, Navien, Rinnai and others, both tank and tankless. See water heater repair and replacement.

Do I need a new water heater or just a repair?

Age is the best guide. If it's under 8 years old and one part failed, repair it. If it's over 10 to 12 years old, or the tank itself is leaking, replace it. In between, we'll price both and let you decide.

Can you find a leak without tearing up my walls?

Usually, yes. We use acoustic listening gear and thermal cameras to pinpoint the leak first, so we only open the wall or floor in one spot. See leak detection.

What causes drains to clog?

Grease in kitchen drains, hair and soap in bathroom drains, and tree roots or mineral scale in older sewer lines. We clear clogs with a cable machine or hydro-jetter and can run a camera to find the cause. See drain cleaning.

Your visit

Do I need to be home?

An adult should be there when we arrive and when we finish, so we can go over the price and the work. Landlords and property managers can arrange access for tenants. We text you about 30 minutes before we arrive.

Will you fix it in one visit?

Most of the time. Our trucks carry the common parts. If a special part has to be ordered, we'll tell you before we start and make the problem safe in the meantime.

Still have a question?

Call any time and talk to a real person, or send a message and we'll get back to you.
