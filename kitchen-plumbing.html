A tip for Austin kitchens

Hard water builds up in faucet aerators and dishwasher valves. If your faucet's flow gets weak, unscrew the aerator at the tip and soak it in vinegar overnight. If that doesn't help, the cartridge inside may be scaled up, and we can replace it.

Common questions

Should I repair or replace my garbage disposal?

If it hums but won't spin, it's usually jammed and we can free it on a service call. If it leaks from the body or it's more than 10 years old, a new one is the better buy.

Can you install a faucet I bought?

Yes. Pick whatever you like and we'll install it at our labor-only rate. If your supply lines are old, we'll suggest replacing them while we're there.

What shouldn't go down the disposal?

Grease, coffee grounds, eggshells, pasta, rice and stringy food like celery and corn husks. Those cause most of the kitchen clogs we see.
